[Verse 1]
You came with thunder in the hold,
and warmer weather from the past,
your sun was tangled in the mast,
and raindrops rolling from your coat.
I never asked which storm was true,
which distant sky should bear your name;
I met your clouds and made no claim.
I knew enough to turn to you.

I woke where older lanterns call,

their lamps are burning by my side,
we all leave marks upon the page.
