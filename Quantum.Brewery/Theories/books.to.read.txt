The closest OpenAI equivalent to Anthropic’s Skilljar is **[OpenAI Academy](https://academy.openai.com/)**. OpenAI’s own learning hub identifies it as the place for courses and AI-learning resources. [ChatGPT Learn](https://learn.chatgpt.com/resources?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

But for you, Archy, I’d begin with this entirely free route:

1. **[ChatGPT Training](https://learn.chatgpt.com/training)**  
   Guided, hands-on training for ChatGPT Work and Codex, from first tasks to practical walkthroughs. Best clean entry point. [learn.chatgpt.com](https://learn.chatgpt.com/training?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

2. **[ChatGPT and Codex documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs)**  
   Start with:

   - [Prompting](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/prompting)
   - [Model selection](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/model-selection)
   - personalization, permissions, projects, skills/plugins, long-running work, and Codex workflows

   This is free reference material rather than a traditional linear course. [ChatGPT Learn](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

3. **[OpenAI Developers: Learning tracks](https://developers.openai.com/learn)**  
   Three structured tracks are currently offered:

   - **Building agents**
   - **AI app development: concept to production**
   - **Model optimization**

   They collect official guides, videos, starter applications, and examples. For your interests, I’d start with **Building agents**. [OpenAI Developers](https://developers.openai.com/learn?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

4. **[Agents learning shelf](https://developers.openai.com/learn/agents)**  
   This is particularly relevant to our Tuesday work: Responses API, tools, orchestration, guardrails, tracing, conversation state, multi-agent patterns, and Agents SDK material. [OpenAI Developers](https://developers.openai.com/learn/agents?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

5. **[OpenAI Cookbook](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook)**  
   The practical laboratory: working recipes for Codex, agents, Responses, tool use, security, and evals. I’d bookmark its [Agents](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/topic/agents) and [Evals](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/topic/evals) collections. [developers.openai.com](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

6. **[Official videos](https://learn.chatgpt.com/videos)**  
   Free demos and walkthroughs covering Codex, agent building, API work, code review, Work features, and practical workflows. This is the easiest “coffee and watch” shelf. [learn.chatgpt.com](https://learn.chatgpt.com/videos?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
