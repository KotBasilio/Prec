  The four serial Stream lanes and visible Impression shell are field-tested. On

  `ImpressionRetinalBridge` owns no thread. Its synchronized methods execute on

  ITROBOC is not flight-control software and does not claim zero allocation.
  However, the live TD camera experience should follow the same useful instinct:
  
  > Prepare, size, and stabilize large resources while the application is
  > grounded; do not ask for another wing during a difficult frame.
